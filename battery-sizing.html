Mathematical model Formulas of Steps 0–6 and selected battery data

Step 0 — Load per UPS

N_GPU = N_GPU,feeder / N_UPS,feeder
check: N_GPU × P_prov ≤ S × PF
P_b   = P_EDP / η           (battery-side power per rack)

Step 1 — Autonomy (Ampace logic)

P_DC   = S × PF / η
N_min  = CEILING(P_DC × t_EOL / (60 × E_mod))
N_rack = MAX( CEILING(N_min / mpr) ;
              CEILING(P_DC×1000 / (mpr×n_cell×V_cell,min × I_lim)) )
N_tot  = N_rack × mpr          n_cell = cells per module (library)
V_min  = mpr×n_cell×V_cell,min      V_max = mpr×n_cell×V_cell,max
t_EOL  = N_tot × E_mod / P_DC × 60      t_BOL = t_EOL / SOH_EOL
I_rack = P_DC×1000 / (N_tot×n_cell×V_cell,min)
OK = V_min > V_UPS,min AND V_max < V_UPS,max AND t_EOL ≥ t_req
     AND I_rack ≤ I_lim AND 3-wire
Recommended = min N_tot, then min N_rack

Step 2 — Ageing

base ageing (EOL SOH of the battery) already in E_mod of Step 1
k_age = 1 + extra % for AI cycling        (applied)
N_req = CEILING(N_tot,1 × k_age)
→ raise modules/rack first (same racks), then racks
k_AI  = SOH_EOL / SOH_AI(cycles/day, year 10)
check: t_EOL,2 × SOH_AI / SOH_EOL ≥ t_req

Step 3 — Ramp-up / ramp-down

E_up       = P_b / C_dis
E_down     = k_ch × P_b / C_ch
E_ramp     = N_GPU × MAX(E_up ; E_down)
E_cab      = mpr × E_rated
N_cab,ramp = CEILING(E_ramp / E_cab)
energy check: E = N_GPU × ΔP × T_r / 2,  T_r = (ΔP/P_b) / r

Step 4 — Power smoothing

P_avg = D × P_EDP + (1−D) × P_idle
E_dis = (P_EDP − P_avg) × D × T
E_ch  = (P_avg − P_idle) × (1−D) × T
N_cab,smooth = 0 if N_GPU × E_dis ≤ spare EOL energy
drift (smoothing mode) = D×(P_max−P_avg) − (1−D)×MIN(P_avg−P_min ; k_ch×S)

Steps 5–6 — Total and checks

N_cab = N_rack,2 + N_cab,ramp + N_cab,smooth   (same mpr)
N_cab ≤ N_parallel,max
N_cab × mpr × P_module ≥ P_DC
C_ch × E_inst ≥ k_ch × S × PF
SOC × E_EOL ≥ P_DC × t_EOL / 60
(1 − SOC) × E_inst ≥ E_ramp-down
